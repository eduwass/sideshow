Publisher identity by default. Saving an identity header on a publication can also make it the workspace default, so new publications carry it without a trip to the dashboard. Every share offers a quick switch: a "Show my identity" tick row in the card's Share menu and a checkbox in the publish-session dialog, remembered as the default for next time. The public page's light/dark toggle and "Add a note" button use lucide icons.
