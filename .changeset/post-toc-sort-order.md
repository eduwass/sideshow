Navigate long sessions. The session header's stream/timeline switch is replaced by a sort toggle (oldest first or newest first, remembered per browser), so fresh posts can land at the top. Sessions with four or more posts get a table of contents on the right: a rail of tick marks that expands on hover or from the keyboard (→ from the stream, ↑/↓ to jump between posts, ⌘↑/⌘↓ for the ends, ←/Esc back), with the post in view showing when it was last updated. ⌘B toggles the sidebar. The experimental timeline view is removed. The document itself no longer scrolls (only the stream does), fixing a blank page appearing below the app after a surface scrolled its ancestors.
