The client-facing publication page gains a local light/dark override — the reader's system scheme by default, their choice remembered on their device only, with each sandboxed surface re-rendered in the matching mode rather than merely restyled. Owners can turn on an optional identity header (avatar, name and one link, off by default) from the publications dashboard, and its avatar is served publicly alongside the publication's own assets.
