Manage share links from a publications dashboard: create generated (unguessable) or custom-slug links, label them for a recipient, set or clear a password and an expiry, turn tracking on or off, copy or open the URL, duplicate a link for another recipient, and revoke one. A duplicate carries the same access settings but is a wholly separate capability — its own analytics, feedback, password, expiry and revocation — so cutting one recipient off never touches another. Expired and revoked links fail closed and are indistinguishable from an unknown link, recipient labels stay owner-only, and a custom slug is labelled in the UI as what it is: an address, not a secret.
